The Lantern Library of Kellsmoor

Kellsmoor is a fictional harbour town invented for this test page. Its best-known building is the Lantern Library, which lends lanterns instead of books. Ada Wren, a ferry pilot, founded it in 1911 so that people could walk the cliff path safely after dark.

Today the library keeps 240 lanterns. Each carries a brass tag with a number and the name of the person who gave it. A lantern may be borrowed for nine nights, and a late return is forgiven if the borrower brings back a story about where the lantern went.

The library is closed on Wednesdays, when volunteers clean the glass and trim the wicks. On the longest night of the year, 21 December, every lantern is lit at once and carried along the harbour wall in a procession called the Long Walk.

Kurz auf Deutsch: Die Laternenbibliothek im erfundenen Hafenstädtchen Kellsmoor verleiht seit 1911 Laternen statt Bücher. Sie besitzt 240 Laternen, ist mittwochs geschlossen und feiert am 21. Dezember den „Langen Gang“.
